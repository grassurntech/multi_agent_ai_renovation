# Plans

How execution plans work.

- New multi-step work gets a plan in `exec-plans/active/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.md`.
- A plan holds: goal, steps (checkboxes), decisions made, open questions.
- When shipped, move it to `exec-plans/completed/` and add a one-line outcome at the top.
- Debt discovered along the way goes to `exec-plans/tech-debt-tracker.md`.
